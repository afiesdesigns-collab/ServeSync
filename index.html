    Case Status Timeline

    Every status explains what's happening, who is responsible, and what happens next. Status is never communicated by color alone — each stage has a text label.

    1. ✓Complete

      Issue reported

      Your case was received and assigned a case number.

    2. 2In progress

      Case reviewed

      A support agent is reviewing your equipment and service history.

    3. 3Upcoming

      Troubleshooting in progress

      The agent will attempt to resolve this remotely.

    4. 4Upcoming

      Technician assigned

      A certified technician will be matched to this job.

    5. 5Upcoming

      Appointment confirmed

      You'll receive a confirmed service window.

    6. 6Upcoming

      Technician en route

      You'll get a real-time arrival update.

    7. 7Upcoming

      Repair in progress

      The technician is on site working the case.

    8. 8Upcoming

      Resolution pending confirmation

      We'll ask you to confirm the equipment works.

    9. 9Upcoming

      Case closed

      Your feedback is recorded and linked to this equipment's history.
